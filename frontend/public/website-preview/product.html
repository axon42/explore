A few useful details

Room for questions.

Does everyone in an interview need an Explore account?

No. Interview participants and app users are different. A customer can be named in the interview without receiving access to the research workspace.

What does the current prototype run on?

The workspace runs in a browser. Live capture currently uses a local macOS helper for microphone and system audio, with Deepgram transcription and a separate analysis provider. Capture reliability is still being refined; it is not ready for public rollout.

Does this include market research?

Not yet. A manually triggered post-meeting market scan is on the roadmap, with its own budget, citations and competitor comparison. It stays separate from what the customer actually said.

Where are signup and pricing?

This is a design review. Accounts, team invitations, hosted access and commercial pricing are still planned. This mock collects no information and makes no purchases.
